Orange and Fennel Salad Recipe

Jump to recipe

Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 large orange, sliced
  • 2 fennel bulb or 1 cup of fennel thinly sliced.
  • half of a lemon
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1/2 lemon squeezed
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • salt and pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Thinly slice fennel bulb using a mandolin or large knife. Ensure that slices are as thin as possible. Finely dice up the leaves and add on top. Place fennel on a large plate or shallow bowl
  2. Slice your orange, removing as much of the pith as possible.
  3. Combine lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper and whisk in a small bowl until well combined.
  4. Place orange slices on top of sliced fennel.
  5. pour the lemon dressing over the salad. Serve
