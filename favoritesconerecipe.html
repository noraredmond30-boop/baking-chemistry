Favorite Scone Recipe

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2/3 cup milk + 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 cup butter (reduced to 7/8 cup)
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp sugar (originally 1/4 cup)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Bake for 12–15 minutes.

Notes

  • Good flavor, but spread too much and wasn’t dense enough.
  • Try 1/4 cup sugar and 2.5 tbsp baking soda next time.
  • Variation: omit sugar, add 1/4 cup spinach, 1/4 cup chocolate chips, 1/4 cup blueberries, and 1/4 cup butter (cut finely). Butter may have been too cold; try 1/8 cup chocolate chips and more blueberries next time.
  • Approx. 1 yogurt cup filled with milk + 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar. Add 1/2 cup chocolate, spinach, 2 tsp flax, and 1/2 tbsp coconut sugar. Divide scones into 2×9 = 18 pieces. Add sunflower seeds to half the batch.
