The science of yeast

Although many people don't know this, yeast is a fungus.When yeast is mixed with warm water and a source of sugar, it begins a process called fermentation. During fermentation, yeast consumes sugars and produces carbon dioxide gas and small amounts of alcohol. The carbon dioxide becomes trapped within the elastic gluten network of the dough, causing the dough to expand and rise. As the dough bakes, the heat kills the yeast, the gases expand further, and the dough sets into a light, airy structure. In addition to making bread rise, fermentation creates many of the complex flavors and aromas that give freshly baked bread its distinctive taste.

Recipe

Ingredients

  • 3.5 cups AP flour
  • 1.25 cups warm water
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1.5 tsp salt
  • 1 package instant yeast. Fleshmans pizza yeast

Directions

  1. Add ingredients into a larg bowl. Mix with a spatula until most of the flour is incorporated then kneed by hand until fully incorporated
  2. Let dough rise 1-2 hours(until dough has doubled in size). Punch down dough. Halve dough
  3. On a well-floured surface shape dough into 10-12 inch disk.
  4. add sauce and toppings
  5. Bake at 500 for 8-10m

Why do we need to activat yeast? is all yeast the same

Many recipes begin by "activating" yeast in warm water, but activation is not always necessary. Active dry yeast has a protective coating that makes it dormant, so soaking it in warm water helps wake the yeast cells and confirms that they are still alive. If bubbles and foam form, the yeast is producing carbon dioxide and is ready to use. Instant yeast, however, consists of smaller granules that absorb water quickly and can usually be mixed directly with the flour without prior activation.

Not all yeast is the same. Bakers commonly use active dry yeast, instant yeast, and fresh yeast. While all three are made from the same species of microorganism, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, they are processed differently. Active dry yeast works more slowly and is often activated first. Instant yeast acts faster and can be added directly to dry ingredients. Fresh yeast contains more moisture and is prized by some bakers for its flavor, but it has a much shorter shelf life. Because these forms of yeast behave differently, recipes may require different amounts or rising times depending on the type used.
